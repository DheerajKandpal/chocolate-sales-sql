# 🍫 Chocolate Sales SQL Analysis

This project showcases my ability to clean, analyze, and extract insights from sales data using SQL in PostgreSQL. I worked with a real-world business dataset to answer key performance questions using aggregation, date functions, filtering, and window functions.

---

## 📂 Dataset Overview

**Table:** `chocolate_sales`  
This dataset contains 6 columns:

- `sales_person` – name of the person who made the sale  
- `country` – country where the sale occurred  
- `product` – name of the chocolate product sold  
- `sale_date` – date of the sale (YYYY-MM-DD format)  
- `amount` – total revenue generated  
- `boxes_shipped` – number of boxes delivered in the sale  

---

## 🔍 Business Questions Answered (SQL Queries)

✅ Total revenue by country  
✅ Top 3 best-selling products  
✅ Monthly revenue trend  
✅ Best performing sales person  
✅ Average revenue per box shipped  
✅ Top-selling product per country  
✅ Revenue share by sales person (%)  
✅ Products with average revenue per box > ₹500
